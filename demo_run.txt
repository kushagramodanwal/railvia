  5. WHAT IS AND IS NOT REAL
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  REAL      section geometry, hourly traffic, day-of-week variation
            (published Indian Railways timetable via RailRadar)
  SYNTHETIC maintenance backlog, crew strength, failure history
            (TMS/SMMS/TDMS have no public equivalent)
  NOT DONE  freight paths are absent from public timetables, so
            night traffic is undercounted — the one place our
            figures could flatter us (A-04)
  CAVEAT    the failure hazard the model learns is one we wrote (A-08)
